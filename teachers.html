Why it matters

Great teachers are the whole secret.

We hire for warmth and hire for craft — then invest in both. Every Little Wonders teacher holds a degree in early childhood education, is CPR and first-aid certified, and completes 40+ hours of professional development each year.

  • 100% of lead teachers hold a degree in early childhood education
  • All staff background-checked and fingerprinted per NYC regulations
  • Annual Montessori, Reggio Emilia, and trauma-informed training
  • Teacher-to-child ratio never exceeds 1:6
