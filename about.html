About KIDER

A trusted childcare center nurturing young minds since 2014. Discover our story, our team, and what makes us different.

📖 Our Story

From a Simple Dream to a Thriving Center

KIDER was founded in 2014 by Maria Santos, a passionate early childhood educator who dreamed of creating a childcare center that truly felt like home. Starting with just 10 children and a small team, KIDER has grown into a vibrant community of over 150 families.

Our name, KIDER, is a playful blend of "kids" and "ider" (a nod to guidance), reflecting our mission to guide children through their most formative years with care, creativity, and respect for each child's unique personality.

Over the past 12 years, we have stayed true to our founding principles: every child deserves safety, warmth, and the opportunity to discover the world at their own pace.

💫 Our Values

What We Believe In

Everything we do is rooted in our commitment to the well-being and development of every child in our care.

💛

Loving & Safe

We create a nurturing environment where every child feels safe, valued, and loved. Safety protocols, low ratios, and warm interactions are at the heart of everything we do.

🎨

Creative & Fun

Learning happens best through play. Our curriculum blends structured activities with open-ended exploration, sparking imagination and building confidence.

🤝

Inclusive & Respectful

We celebrate diversity and teach children to appreciate differences. Every family is welcomed, every background is honored, and every child is included.
