Who we are

A home away from home since 2012.

What started as two teachers and eight children in a converted parlor has grown into one of Brooklyn's most loved preschools. What hasn't changed is our philosophy: children learn best through play, through relationships, and through wonder.

Our program is built on the Montessori and Reggio Emilia traditions, adapted for modern families. We keep classes small, teachers certified, and the garden full of tomatoes.

  • Licensed by the NYC Department of Health
  • Member of NAEYC (National Association for the Education of Young Children)
  • 1:6 teacher-to-child ratio in every classroom
  • On-site chef preparing fresh, organic meals daily
