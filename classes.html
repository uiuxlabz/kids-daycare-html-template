Tuition & enrollment

Simple, transparent pricing.

Tuition includes all meals, snacks, materials, field trips, and the daily garden program. No hidden fees, ever. Financial assistance and sibling discounts are available for qualifying families.

  • Flexible schedules — 3, 4, or 5 days per week
