6–18 Months

Tiny Explorers

Our Tiny Explorers program provides a warm, responsive environment for our youngest learners. Every interaction is an opportunity for discovery and connection.

  • Low 1:3 staff-to-child ratio for personalized attention
  • Sensory-rich play stations designed for developmental milestones
  • Daily music, movement, and story sessions
  • Flexible feeding and nap schedules honoring each child's rhythm
  • Daily photo and milestone updates for parents
Enroll in This Program
18–24 Months

Curious Crawlers

Curious Crawlers is designed for toddlers who are on the move. Our curriculum encourages safe exploration and builds early communication skills through hands-on activities.

  • Interactive play zones for gross and fine motor development
  • Early language development through songs and conversations
  • Guided art and sensory activities daily
  • Safe outdoor exploration and nature walks
  • Gentle introduction to routines and group activities
Enroll in This Program
2–3 Years

Little Adventurers

Little Adventurers builds on natural curiosity through dramatic play, creative expression, and social skill development in a structured yet playful environment.

  • Social-emotional learning through guided group activities
  • Early literacy and numeracy foundations through play
  • Creative art studio sessions twice weekly
  • Music, dance, and movement classes
  • Garden and nature exploration time
Enroll in This Program
3–4 Years

Creative Thinkers

Creative Thinkers nurtures independent thinking and problem-solving through a balanced curriculum of STEM exploration, literacy, and creative arts.

  • Hands-on STEM experiments and discoveries
  • Pre-reading and pre-writing skill development
  • Weekly art studio with professional materials
  • Science exploration garden and outdoor lab
  • Collaborative projects building teamwork skills
Enroll in This Program
4–5 Years

Big Dreamers

Our flagship pre-kindergarten program gives children the academic and social-emotional tools they need to transition confidently into formal schooling.

  • Kindergarten readiness curriculum aligned with state standards
  • Reading and math foundations through engaging activities
  • Weekly field trips to community locations
  • Leadership and independence skill building
  • Portfolio-based progress tracking shared with parents
Enroll in This Program
5–8 Years

After-School Explorers

Our after-school program provides a safe, enriching environment for school-age children with homework support, sports, and creative enrichment activities.

  • Structured homework help with qualified tutors
  • Daily outdoor sports and physical activities
  • Cooking and gardening enrichment clubs
  • STEM challenge projects and maker activities
  • Flexible pickup times to match school schedules
